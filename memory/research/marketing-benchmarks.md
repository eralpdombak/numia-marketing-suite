## 💰 COST BENCHMARKS

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

**B2B SaaS Averages:**
- SMB: $200-$500
- Mid-Market: $1,000-$5,000
- Enterprise: $10,000-$50,000

**Crypto-Specific (Estimated):**
- Developer tier ($50-$200/month): CAC $100-$300
- Protocol tier ($500-$2K/month): CAC $500-$2,000
- Enterprise ($5K+/month): CAC $2,000-$10,000

**CAC Payback Period:**
- Target: <12 months
- Good: <6 months
- Excellent: <3 months

### LTV:CAC Ratio

**Industry Standard:**
- Minimum viable: 3:1
- Good: 4:1-5:1
- Excellent: >5:1

**Formula:**
```
LTV = (Average Revenue Per Account × Gross Margin) / Revenue Churn Rate
LTV:CAC = LTV / CAC
```

**Example for Numia:**
- Average Pro user: $149/month
- Gross margin: 80%
- Churn: 5%/month
- LTV = ($149 × 0.8) / 0.05 = $2,384
- If CAC = $500
- LTV:CAC = $2,384 / $500 = 4.8:1 ✅ Good ratio

## 🎤 CONTENT MARKETING ROI

### Content Multiplier Effect

**Research Finding:** "Content-driven SEO drives 1000% more qualified traffic than social media"

**Meaning:**
- 1,000 Twitter followers = 100 website visits/month
- 1 good blog post ranking #1 = 1,000+ website visits/month
- **Implication:** Invest in SEO content, not just social

### Time Investment vs. Return

**Blog Post:**
- Time to create: 4-8 hours
- Lifespan: 2-3 years (with updates)
- Traffic per year: 1,000-10,000 visits
- ROI: 500-5,000 views per hour invested

**Twitter Thread:**
- Time to create: 30-60 minutes
- Lifespan: 24-48 hours
- Impressions: 500-10,000
- ROI: 500-10,000 impressions per hour invested

**Both are valuable** - threads for immediate engagement, blogs for long-term SEO.

## 🏆 VIRAL LOOP BENCHMARKS

### K-Factor (Virality Coefficient)

**Definition:** Average number of new users each user brings

**Benchmarks:**
- K < 1: Not viral (normal growth)
- K = 1: Breaking even (each user brings 1 more)
- K > 1: Viral growth (exponential)
- K = 1.5: Excellent virality

**Famous Examples:**
- Dropbox: K = 1.3-1.5 (3900% growth from referrals)
- Airbnb: K = 1.2-1.4
- Uber: K = 1.1-1.3

**Numia Target:** K = 1.2-1.3 (achievable with good referral program)

### Referral Conversion Rates

**Typical Referral Program:**
- % of users who refer: 5-15%
- % of referred who sign up: 20-40%
- % of referred who convert to paid: 15-30% (higher than organic)

**Numia Target:**
- 10% of users refer (via dashboard sharing)
- 30% of referred sign up
- 25% of referred convert to paid

## 🚨 WARNING SIGNALS

**When to Be Concerned:**

❌ **Conversion < 5%** (free to paid) after Month 3
❌ **Churn > 7%/month** consistently
❌ **CAC Payback > 18 months**
❌ **Day 7 retention < 20%**
❌ **Blog traffic flat for 6+ months**
❌ **Twitter growth < 100/month** after Month 3

**What to Do:**
- If conversion low → Improve onboarding + product value
- If churn high → Interview churned customers, fix product gaps
- If CAC high → Optimize channels, focus on organic
- If retention low → Find "aha moment," optimize activation
- If content flat → SEO audit, more promotion
- If Twitter slow → Increase engagement, better content
