# Instagram Post Guidelines

## Brand Voice for Instagram
- Visual-first platform - think about the image before the caption
- Shorter, punchier than LinkedIn
- More casual but still professional
- Use line breaks for readability
- Can be slightly more provocative/edgy than LinkedIn

## Format Structure

### Caption Format:
1. **Hook** (first line - appears in feed preview)
2. **Body** (2-4 short paragraphs, use line breaks)
3. **Call to action**
4. **Hashtags** (10-15 relevant hashtags, research-based)

### Image Concept:
- Always include visual concept description
- Think: charts, data visualizations, screenshots, or branded graphics
- Must be eye-catching and stop the scroll

## Content Rules

**DO:**
- Lead with a strong hook (question, bold statement, or surprising fact)
- Use emojis strategically (1-3 per post, not excessive)
- Include specific data points and numbers
- Add line breaks between paragraphs for mobile readability
- End with clear CTA (comment, share, visit link in bio, DM)
- Use relevant hashtags (mix of popular + niche)

**DON'T:**
- Write walls of text (use line breaks!)
- Be too corporate or formal
- Use more than 15 hashtags (looks spammy)
- Forget the visual component
- Make it all about you (provide value first)

## Length
- Caption: 125-150 words max
- First line (hook): Under 125 characters (fits in preview)
- Hashtags: 10-15 total

## Hashtag Strategy
- Mix of sizes:
  - Large (1M+ posts): 2-3 hashtags
  - Medium (100K-1M posts): 5-7 hashtags
  - Small (10K-100K posts): 3-5 hashtags
- Industry-specific: blockchain, crypto, data, analytics
- Branded: #Numia, #CosmosEcosystem
- Content-type: #BlockchainAnalytics, #CryptoData

## Visual Concepts
- Data visualizations (charts showing trends)
- Quote cards (pull key stat or insight)
- Behind-the-scenes (team, product development)
- Infographics (simplified complex topics)
- Screenshots (product features, dashboards)
- Memes (if brand-appropriate and tasteful)

## Engagement Tactics
- Ask questions in caption
- Use "Save this post" hooks for educational content
- "Tag someone who needs to see this"
- Polls in stories (link to feed post)
- Carousel posts for step-by-step or data breakdowns

## Tone Examples

**Good:**
- "IBC just processed 1M transactions. Here's why that matters 👇"
- "Everyone talks about Ethereum gas fees. But Cosmos fees? *chef's kiss* 💸"
- "3 data points that changed how we think about cross-chain analytics:"

**Bad:**
- "We are pleased to announce our latest feature update..." (too corporate)
- "Check out our blog post about blockchain stuff" (too vague)
- Long paragraphs with no breaks (hard to read on mobile)

## Call to Action Examples
- "Drop a 🔥 if you're building on Cosmos"
- "Link in bio for the full breakdown"
- "Save this for later"
- "What chain should we cover next? Comment below"
- "DM us for early access"

## Platform Specifics
- Instagram favors engagement (comments, saves, shares)
- Post at optimal times (varies by audience, test and learn)
- Use stories to drive traffic to feed posts
- Reels for maximum reach (but maintain quality)
- Consistent aesthetic/theme helps brand recognition

## Remember
- Instagram is visual storytelling
- The image does 70% of the work
- Caption should complement, not compete with image
- Mobile-first: everything must look good on phone
- Value first, promotion second
