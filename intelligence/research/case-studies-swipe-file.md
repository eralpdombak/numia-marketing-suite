**Numia Adaptation:**

## 📊 WHAT DIDN'T WORK - FAILURES TO AVOID

### Terra/UST - Why It Failed

**Context:** Built on Cosmos, catastrophically collapsed May 2022

**What Went Wrong:**
- Unsustainable tokenomics (20% yield impossible)
- Founder hubris (Do Kwon's arrogance)
- Ignored warning signs
- Lack of transparency

**Lessons for Numia:**
- Sustainable business model > growth at all costs
- Humility in marketing (no arrogance)
- Transparent about limitations
- Listen to criticism

---

### Cosmos Project Leadership Crisis

**What Happened:**
- Jae Kwon stepped down (2020)
- 10 engineers left in protest
- Infighting between factions
- "Existential crisis" headlines

**Lessons for Numia:**
- Team stability matters
- Avoid public infighting
- Don't let founders become liability
- Communication > Ego

---

### Flipside Crypto - Product Shutdown

**What Went Wrong:**
- Tried to compete directly with Dune (bad strategy)
- Split focus (Snowflake enterprise vs. community)
- Product too complex
- Poor monetization (waited too long)

**Lessons for Numia:**
- Don't compete with Dune head-on (focus on Cosmos)
- Pick ONE customer segment to nail first
- Keep product simple
- Monetize early (don't wait for Series B)
