# Writing Process & Quality Control

## Writing Process (How to Actually Do This)

### Step 1: Get the Raw Draft

If using AI:
- Give it detailed prompts with examples
- Treat the output as a rough outline only
- Never publish AI output directly

### Step 2: Humanize It

1. Read out loud - Does it sound like you?
2. Add specifics - Replace every vague statement
3. Break rules - Add fragments, start with And/But
4. Inject personality - Opinions, emotion, humor
5. Vary rhythm - Mix long and short sentences
6. Add contractions - Every single "do not" becomes "don't"
7. Cut 30% - Be ruthless
8. Remove AI tells - Check the banned words list
9. Add scenarios - Replace concepts with stories
10. Test the opener - Would this hook you?

### Step 3: The Quality Checklist

Before publishing, verify:
- ☐ Would I actually say this out loud?
- ☐ Is there a clear point/benefit in the first 2 sentences?
- ☐ Did I cut all corporate jargon and AI clichés?
- ☐ Are there specific examples (not vague concepts)?
- ☐ Does it sound like a human with personality wrote it?
- ☐ Would someone actually want to read this?
- ☐ Is every word necessary?
- ☐ Did I avoid all words from the "AI tells" list?
- ☐ Did I use "you" to speak directly to the reader?
- ☐ Are there at least 3 contractions?
- ☐ Did I break at least 2 grammar rules naturally?
- ☐ Is there emotion or personality?

If any answer is NO, fix that part.

## Final Pre-Publishing Checklist

### The 7 Critical Questions

1. **Would I actually say this to a developer I respect?**
   - If no → Too corporate or too casual

2. **Is this about them or about us?**
   - Should be about them 80% of the time

3. **Would someone share this with their team?**
   - If no → Add more value or specificity

4. **Did I use any words from the banned AI clichés list?**
   - If yes → Remove and replace immediately

5. **Is the first sentence about their problem, not our solution?**
   - If no → Rewrite the opener

6. **Does this sound like a human with personality wrote it?**
   - If no → Apply humanization checklist

7. **Is every word necessary?**
   - If no → Cut 30% more

### The Quick Skim Test

Read only the first sentence of each paragraph:
- Does it tell a coherent story?
- Would someone keep reading?
- Is each sentence about the reader, not you?

If no, restructure.

### The Out-Loud Test

Read the entire piece out loud:
- Do you stumble anywhere?
- Does it sound natural?
- Would you say it this way in conversation?

If you stumble, rewrite that part.

### The "Does This Sound Like Us?" Test

Compare to previous best-performing content:
- Same voice and energy?
- Same level of specificity?
- Same mix of technical + conversational?

If it doesn't match your best work, figure out why and fix it.

## Common Mistakes and How to Fix Them

**Mistake 1: Too Generic**
- Problem: Could apply to any company in the space
- Fix: Add specific numbers, names, scenarios

**Mistake 2: Too Corporate**
- Problem: Sounds like a press release
- Fix: Add contractions, break rules, use "you"

**Mistake 3: Too Promotional**
- Problem: Talks about features, not benefits
- Fix: Lead with their pain, solution comes later

**Mistake 4: Too Safe**
- Problem: No opinions, no edges
- Fix: Take a stance, call out problems, be specific

**Mistake 5: Too Long**
- Problem: Rambles, loses the point
- Fix: Cut 30%, make every sentence earn its place

**Mistake 6: No Emotion**
- Problem: Just facts, no feeling
- Fix: Show frustration, relief, excitement—be human

**Mistake 7: AI Tells Everywhere**
- Problem: "Moreover," perfect grammar, generic
- Fix: Break rules, add personality, get specific

## No Hashtags or Numbers in Letters

**Rules:**
- Do not use any hashtags in any content
- Do not write numbers in letters (write "3" not "Three")
- Exception: When the number starts a sentence, you can spell it out, but try to restructure instead

**Examples:**
- ❌ "Three providers" → ✅ "3 providers"
- ❌ "#blockchain #data" → ✅ Delete entirely
- ❌ "Ten thousand requests" → ✅ "10k requests"

## No Meta-Commentary (Critical Rule)

Never:
- Mention "as an AI"
- Explain your content generation process
- Say "I'm going to tell you about..."
- Preview what you're about to say

Just deliver the content naturally as if a skilled human writer created it (because after editing, a human did).
