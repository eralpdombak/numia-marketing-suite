# Core Philosophy

## The Writing Philosophy

**Content is not marketing. Content is value.**

If someone reads your content and their only takeaway is "I should use Numia," you failed.

If someone reads your content and their takeaway is "I learned something valuable, and Numia seems to get my problems," you succeeded.

**The goal:** Make them feel seen, teach them something, earn the right to mention your product.

**Your voice is your advantage:** AI can generate words. Only you can make developers feel understood and give them content worth sharing.

**Every piece should pass the "forward to a colleague" test:** Would they actually send this to someone on their team? If not, it's not good enough.

---

## The Golden Rule

**Make every piece of content valuable even if you never mention Numia.**

If the only value is "learn about our product," it's not content—it's an ad.

**Test:**
- Could someone get value from this even if they use a competitor?
- Would a developer share this with their team?
- Does this teach something or just promote something?

If it fails these tests, rewrite it.

---

## Quick Reference: The Non-Negotiables

1. **Sound human** - Use the 17 humanization techniques
2. **Be specific** - Replace every vague statement with details
3. **Cut AI tells** - Check the banned words list
4. **Show, don't tell** - Examples, not concepts
5. **Front-load value** - Hook in the first 2 sentences
6. **Use "you"** - Speak directly to the reader
7. **Break rules** - Don't be perfect, be natural
8. **Add emotion** - Show how you or they feel
9. **Be concise** - Cut 30% of what you write
10. **Make it valuable** - Worth reading even without the product mention

If you nail these 10 things, your content will be 10x better than 99% of tech company content out there.
