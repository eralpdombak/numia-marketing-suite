Your indexer says 1.3 ETH. Your RPC node says 1.7 ETH. You refresh four times.

**2/**

Here's what's actually happening:

Your RPC node shows real-time state. Your indexer shows processed historical data. They're looking at different points in time, pulling from different sources.

And nobody tells you which one is "right."

**3/**

The sync delay problem:

Block finality: 12 seconds
Indexer processing: 30-90 seconds
Cache invalidation: whenever it feels like it

By the time your dashboard updates, your RPC already moved on to the next block.

**4/**

It gets worse with reorgs.

Your RPC follows the longest chain (even if it reorganizes). Your indexer might still be processing the old chain.

Result? Your balance exists in one reality but not the other.
