Your blockchain indexer is silently dropping 3% of transactions.

You don't know it yet. Your users do.

Here's what's happening: Most indexers assume sequential blocks. But chain reorgs make blocks disappear and reappear with different transactions. Your indexer sees block 1,000,001, marks it done, moves on. 

Then a reorg happens. Block 1,000,001 gets replaced. New transactions in that slot? Never indexed. Just... gone.

You're looking at your dashboard thinking everything's working. Your users are wondering why their transaction from 20 minutes ago still shows as pending. Or worse - shows nothing at all.

The brutal part? This happens more often than you think. Polygon reorgs 2-3 times per hour. Arbitrum, multiple times a day. Even Ethereum has them.

How many transactions is your indexer actually missing? Be honest - have you checked?

Here's what catches it: Track block hashes, not just heights. When a hash changes at the same height, re-index that entire block. Monitor uncle/orphan rates. Set up alerts for missing transaction counts.

Most teams find out they're dropping data when a user files a support ticket. Some never find out at all - just slowly bleed users who think their transactions are "stuck."

Test your indexer right now: Pick a block during a known reorg. Check if you indexed all final transactions. Not the ones that got orphaned - the ones that actually made it to chain.

If you're not explicitly handling reorgs, you're dropping transactions. It's not a question of if, just how many.
